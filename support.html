Support

CanopyChat Support

Need help with CanopyChat? Email support@canopychat.app.

What to Include

  • Your device and operating system: iPhone or Android model and OS version, Mac model and macOS version, or Windows or Linux version (for Linux, your distribution, for example Ubuntu 22.04).
  • The CanopyChat app version (and build number on iPhone, Android or Mac).
  • A short description of what happened.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the problem.

Subscription Help

Subscriptions are managed by the store or account you bought them with. On iPhone and Mac, that is your Apple ID: if a purchase does not appear, tap Restore Purchases in CanopyChat, then check Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android, check Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. On Windows and Linux, and for anything bought on the web, sign in to your CanopyChat account. If you still need help, email us with the email address on your account.

Model Download Help

On-device models and local picture models can be large downloads, and the first setup may take a while. Use a fast connection, keep the app open during first setup when possible, and make sure your device has enough free storage.

Permissions

Location, camera, microphone, photo, and file access can be changed in your device settings: iOS or Android Settings, macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security, or your Windows or Linux privacy settings. CanopyChat asks for these permissions only when a feature needs them.
