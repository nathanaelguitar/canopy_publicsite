Limited enrollment

Growing carefully, one group at a time.

Founding Member enrollment is intentionally limited because the beta is opening in carefully managed groups. That gives us room to listen well, respond thoughtfully, and make sure each member receives a high-quality experience.

When the current group is full, new applicants will be invited to join the waitlist for the next opening.

FAQ

A few helpful details.

When will I receive access?

Invitations are sent in managed groups. After joining, you will receive an email with your invitation and setup steps when your beta group is ready.

How long will the beta last?

There is not a published end date yet. Founding Members will receive clear updates as the beta progresses and advance notice before the experience changes.

Do I have to subscribe later?

No. Founding Membership does not enroll you in a future subscription. When the beta period ends, continuing with a paid Premium plan will be entirely your choice.

What happens if I decide not to continue?

Nothing is charged automatically. You can simply choose not to begin a Premium subscription when the beta concludes.

Can I use the same account later?

That is the intended path. Keep access tied to the same email address, and your invitation will include the exact steps for carrying your Founding Member access forward.
