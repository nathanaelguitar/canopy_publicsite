FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the $10 payment include?

Early beta access when the app becomes available, three months of Premium starting when your access is activated, and a direct way to send feedback while the product is still taking shape. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription.

When will I receive access?

We email your invitation as soon as your beta access is ready. We are not publishing a launch date yet, and we would rather tell you that plainly than guess.

When does my three-month Premium period begin?

On the day your access is activated — not the day you pay. If there is a gap between your payment and your invitation, it does not come out of your three months.

Will I be charged automatically after three months?

No. The $10 is charged once. There is no recurring billing, no automatic renewal, and no stored subscription waiting to start.

Will I need to subscribe later?

Only if you want to keep Premium features after your three months. CanopyChat remains usable without a subscription, and continuing is a decision you make and authorize yourself.

How will I access the app?

CanopyChat is an iPhone app. Your invitation email will include the steps to install the beta and activate your Founding Member access.

Can I use the same account on the website and mobile app?

Your Founding Member access is tied to the email address you use at checkout, and that is the address you use to activate access in the app.

What happens if the beta timeline changes?

We email Founding Members. Your access does not expire while you wait, and your three months of Premium still start when you are let in.
