FAQ

A few helpful details.

When will I receive access?

After your payment is confirmed, we will email your iPhone TestFlight link and setup steps. Premium begins when your beta access is activated.

How long will the beta last?

The Founding Member beta lasts three months. We will keep members informed throughout and provide clear next steps before it ends.

Do I have to subscribe later?

No. Founding Membership does not enroll you in a future subscription. When the three-month beta ends, continuing with a paid Premium plan will be entirely your choice.

What happens if I decide not to continue?

Nothing is charged automatically. You can simply choose not to begin a Premium subscription when the beta concludes.

Can I use the same account later?

That is the intended path. Keep access tied to the same email address, and your invitation will include the exact steps for carrying your Founding Member access forward.

Where is beta enrollment available?

Founding Member enrollment is currently available to customers in the United States.
