# Meet Flame. It gets things done while you’re away.

Flame is the AI agent inside CanopyChat, and it has its own house in the clouds: a private cloud computer that keeps working after you close your laptop. Tell it what you need, then check in from your phone whenever you like.

## An agent that keeps you in the loop.

You stay in charge of where Flame works and what it keeps. It does the legwork.

**Has its own place in the cloud**

Flame gets a private cloud computer of its own. Shut your laptop, catch your flight, go to sleep. Flame keeps working.

**Works where you work**

Bring in the projects you want help with from your Mac or PC. Flame only works with what you share with it.

**Tells you as it goes**

No black box. Flame reports what it’s doing step by step, and you can follow along from your phone.

**Answers from anywhere**

Message Flame from your phone wherever you are, and come back to finished work instead of a stalled task.

**Remembers, if you want it to**

Persistent memory is opt-in. Turn it on and Flame remembers your projects and preferences; leave it off and every task starts fresh.

**Goes by your name for it**

Flame is the default. Call it whatever you like.
